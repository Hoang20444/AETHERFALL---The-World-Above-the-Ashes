# Object Reasoning & Visual Craft Guide

When creating or modifying 3D assets for stylized fantasy worlds:

## 1. Silhouette & Readability
- An asset must communicate its function through its outer contour alone.
- Test: View the object in pure unlit black silhouette against a white background.
- If shapes merge into an unrecognizable blob, increase negative space:
  - Separate arms and weapons from character torso.
  - Exaggerate cape and scarf flair.
  - Flay building buttresses outward.
  - Add distinct crenellations, spire spires, or floating fragments.

## 2. Stylized Fantasy Geometry Rules
- **Bevel all hard 90-degree edges:** Real and fantasy stone is never laser-sharp. Use a 2-segment bevel with `width: 0.05 - 0.15m` and enable smooth shading with auto-smooth angles (30° - 45°).
- **Secondary detail hierarchy:**
  - Macro: Massive block structure (island root, tower shaft).
  - Meso: Inset friezes, cornices, buttresses, stone slabs.
  - Micro: Rune engraving, chipped stone corners, crevice moss.
- **Organic rock roots:** Bottoms of floating islands should feature tapered stalactite-like crystalline formations, weathered striations, and inverted ridges rather than geometric smooth cones.

## 3. Humanoid Proportions for Stylized Game Characters
- **Head to Body Ratio:** 1 : 6.5 to 1 : 7 (Heroic stylized proportion).
- **Shoulders:** Broadened silhouette via capelet, cowl, or pauldrons.
- **Limbs:** Slightly exaggerated boots and gauntlets for readable footwork and gestures in third-person camera.
- **Layers:** Tunic/Undersuit -> Leather Armor/Vest -> Cloak/Scarf/Satchel -> Equipment straps.
